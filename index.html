Deal Flow Radar

A personal research tool that turns business-marketplace alert emails into a weekly digest of software niches worth building in.

What it does

Deal Flow Radar is a private, single-user application run by its owner on their own computer. Once a week it:

  • reads, with read-only access, the emails in one Gmail label that the owner has set up for alerts from business marketplaces such as Acquire.com;
  • extracts the listed facts about businesses for sale (for example revenue, asking price and product type);
  • groups similar businesses into product niches and scores them;
  • writes a weekly report that stays on the owner's computer.

What it does not do

  • It never sends, deletes, modifies or labels email.
  • It never reads mail outside the single label the owner chose.
  • It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts; it is used only by its owner.
